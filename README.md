## Hi, I'm Ben 👋

I'm a product leader in Colorado, currently **Head of Product at [Join](https://join.build/)**, where we build preconstruction software for the teams that plan and price large construction projects. Before that I spent years in enterprise product at **Workday**, and in between I took a "learning sabbatical" to find out whether I could build things myself.

Turns out I can – just not always into a business. That's most of what's in here.

### What I'm actually doing

I'm a PM who writes code. Not production code that anyone should depend on – but enough to prototype an idea over a weekend, to have an informed opinion in an architecture conversation, and to stop treating engineering estimates as a black box. Most of what I build now is built *with* AI agents rather than around them, which has changed how I think about product work more than any framework I've read.

### What I build

The through-line: I keep building the tool I personally needed and couldn't buy. Most of it is agent-driven systems for taming information overload – pipelines that ingest the firehose of newsletters and articles I subscribe to, build a knowledge base out of it, and hand it back to me in a form I'll actually consume. The rest is small, sharp tools for the people problems I've hit as a manager – feedback, gratitude, the decisions teams make and the consequences they live with. A few were real attempts at a business and I shut them down when the model didn't work. Others exist because a weekend was available. Shipping and shutting down are the same skill, and I'd rather show both.

### What I like talking about

Product discipline on small teams. Where AI agents actually change the job of a PM versus where they just add a chat box. Vertical software. Why "outcomes over activities" is easy to say and very hard to actually hold a team to.
